Stylist notes

Clean the white sneakers

Your white leather pair is scuffed and grey at the toes. Ten minutes with a magic eraser makes every outfit here look sharper.

The one gap: a dark shoe

All four pairs are white. When you next buy anything, make it brown suede Chelsea boots or loafers. They'd upgrade every chinos and knit outfit.

Gym tees stay at the gym

Deadpool, N64, the 03 ringer and both Bears tees only show up in Gym looks. Keep them there.

The rules behind the combos

One denim piece max

Denim shirt, sherpa jacket and medium jeans never go together.

Layer in contrast

A jacket and the top under it are never the same colour.

No muddy greens

Green, olive and sage never sit next to each other.

Keep a dark anchor near beige

Oatmeal, stone and sage need something dark in the outfit so they don't wash out your skin.

Flannel over plain tops

The plaid only goes over tees and knits, never over another collared shirt.

Graphic tees stay casual

Big-print tees only go with jeans or cargos, never chinos or ivory. Cargos only go with tees and hoodies.

Caps stay casual

Caps only go on summer looks and outfits with a tee, hoodie, denim jacket, flannel or heavy jacket. The navy cap never goes with another navy piece.

Weekend tag

Outfits with hoodies, graphic tees, cargos, light-wash denim or the denim jacket are marked Weekend. Use "Grown-up only" to hide them.

Shoes follow the colours

Earthy or denim outfits get the Levi's sneakers with the tan heel. Black, white, grey and navy get the white leather pair. Light summer looks get the slip-ons. The gym always gets the Adidas runners.

One busy thing at the gym

The wash-print shorts only go with plain tops. Shorts get white crew socks, joggers get black ankle socks.

Weather-sensible

No short sleeves or white jeans under the puffer. Polos only under a layer when it's cool.
